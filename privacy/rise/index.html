RISE · Privacy

RISE Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

RISE is a block-stacking arcade game made by VitalApps Ltd, a UK-registered company. This policy explains what the app handles and the choices you have. The short version: there is no account, your progress never leaves your device, and we never sell your data.

2. Information We Collect

On your device only

  • Game progress — your high score, best score without a revive, games played, blocks stacked, daily streak, hearts and gems. This is saved in the app's own storage on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot see it.
  • Settings — volume, mute, and haptics preferences, stored locally in the same way.

Collected by third parties

  • Advertising identifier — Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to select adverts. You can reset or limit it in your device settings.
  • Purchase records — when you buy something, Google Play processes the payment and tells the app whether it succeeded. We never see your card details.

What we do not collect

  • No account and no sign-in. RISE has no login, so we hold no email address or name for you.
  • No location. The app never reads your GPS or physical location.
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone access. The app requests only internet access and permission to vibrate.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To save your progress and settings between sessions, on your device.
  • To show adverts and to remove them if you buy the ad-free upgrade.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles ourselves.

4. Data Storage & Security

RISE has no game server. Your progress is written to the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Because it is stored only on your phone, uninstalling the app permanently deletes it, and progress does not transfer to a new device.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — shows banner and rewarded adverts, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Rewarded adverts are always optional and only play when you choose to watch one.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases (the ad-free upgrade and extra lives). Payment details are handled entirely by Google.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because RISE stores everything on your device and nothing on our servers, you are always in full control of your data.

  • To delete your game data from inside the app, open RISE → Settings (gear icon) → Legal → Delete my data, then confirm. This permanently erases your high scores, stats, hearts, day streak and settings from the device straight away.
  • You can also delete it from outside the app by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → RISE → Storage → Clear data on Android) or by uninstalling. Both have the same effect.
  • Your ad-free purchase is not erased by any of the above, because it is an entitlement held by Google Play rather than data we store. It returns automatically when you reinstall or tap Restore Purchases.
  • There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never receive it. If you would like written confirmation of that, email us at the address below and we will reply.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play under Google's own retention policy, not ours.

7. Your Rights (UK / EEA Users)

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or erase personal data a company holds about you. As RISE holds no personal data on our systems, these rights are satisfied automatically — but you are welcome to contact us with any question and we will answer it.

8. Children's Privacy

RISE is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will act promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email vitalappsltd@gmail.com and we will get back to you.
