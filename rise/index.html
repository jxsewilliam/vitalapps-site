What it is

A tower that only goes up if your timing does.

RISE is a one-tap stacking game. There's one control and one decision: when to tap. Every block you land perfectly keeps the tower wide. Every block you miss trims it narrower, until a single mistimed tap ends the run. No tutorial needed — you'll understand it in three seconds and still be chasing your best score a month later.

◆

One tap, nothing else

No buttons, no joystick, no menus mid-run. Tap to drop, tap to restart. The whole game fits in one thumb.

★

Perfect stacks pay off

Land a block dead centre and nothing gets sliced away. String perfects together and the tower stays full-width — that's how the big scores happen.

♥

Hearts to keep the run alive

Spend a heart to carry on from where you fell instead of starting over. You begin with three, and you can always watch a short ad for one more shot.

▲

An honest leaderboard

Your stats track your best score with revives and your best score without one, separately. The no-revive number is the one that actually counts.

◷

Streaks and stats

Games played, blocks stacked, average score, and a day streak that grows every day you come back. Small numbers that make one more run very easy to justify.

✕

Remove ads for good

One small purchase clears the ads permanently. No subscription, no timers, no energy meter — RISE never stops you from playing.

How it works

Three rules. That's the whole game.

01

Tap to drop.

A block swings back and forth above the tower. Tap and it falls exactly where it was.

02

Overhang gets cut.

Anything hanging over the edge is sliced off and falls away. The next block is only as wide as what's left.

03

Miss and it's over.

Trim the tower down to nothing and the run ends. Spend a heart to continue, or start again and beat it properly.
