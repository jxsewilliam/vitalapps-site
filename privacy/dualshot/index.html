DualShot Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This privacy policy describes how DualShot, an iOS application operated by VitalApps Ltd ("we", "our", or "us"), handles information about you when you use the app.

DualShot is a camera app that captures photos and videos using both of your iPhone's lenses at the same time. It is designed to keep your captures on your device and to collect as little personal information as possible.

Permissions DualShot requests

To work, DualShot must request the following permissions. You can review or revoke any of these at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera: Required to capture photos and video using your iPhone's front and rear cameras. Without this permission, the app cannot function.
  • Microphone: Required when you record video, so the captured video has audio.
  • Photo Library (Add Only): Used only to save the photos and videos you capture into your Photos app. We do not read existing photos from your library.

What data DualShot stores

  • Your captures: Photos and videos you take with DualShot are saved to your iOS Photos library and stay on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, ever.
  • App settings: Layout preferences (side-by-side vs. picture-in-picture), aspect ratio, and other in-app settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase status: The fact that you purchased DualShot is managed by Apple's StoreKit. We receive a confirmation that the purchase is valid, but no payment details.

What data we collect from you

  • Anonymous device identifiers: For analytics and crash reporting. These cannot identify you personally.
  • Anonymous usage data: Aggregated, non-identifying information about how the app is used (e.g. which capture mode is most popular), to help us improve DualShot.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or any other personally identifiable information through DualShot.

Photos and videos

This is the most important point: your photos and videos never leave your device through DualShot. We do not upload them, view them, store them, analyse them, or share them with anyone. They are saved straight to your iOS Photos library, which is controlled by you and Apple.

Third-party services

We use Apple's standard analytics and crash reporting tools, which provide only anonymous, aggregated information. DualShot does not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

In-app purchases

DualShot is a paid app. The £9.99 lifetime purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple handles all payment information according to its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can revoke camera, microphone, or photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings → DualShot. You can delete the app at any time, which removes all DualShot-related data from your device. Because your captures live in your Photos library, removing them is your choice and is done in the Photos app.

If you have any privacy questions or requests, contact us at vitalappsltd@gmail.com.

Children

DualShot is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this URL. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
