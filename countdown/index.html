How it works

Three taps and you're done.

01

Scan the barcode.

Point your camera at any product and it's saved to your inventory. About three seconds.

02

Snap the expiry date.

Photograph the date on the label and Countdown reads it. It nudges you before food turns — not after it's in the bin.

03

Eat it. Don't bin it.

Open the app, see what needs eating first, and actually eat it. That's the whole loop.
