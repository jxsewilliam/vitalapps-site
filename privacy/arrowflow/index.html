Arrow Flow Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Arrow Flow: Escape Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by VitalApps Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom. This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Flow handles your information. The short version: we don't collect any personal information about you.

2. Information We Collect

None. Arrow Flow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-ins, no email collection, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising trackers.

Your puzzle progress, completed levels, and personal best times are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad only. They never leave your device.

3. Permissions

Arrow Flow does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, or any other sensitive iOS data.

4. iCloud Backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your Arrow Flow progress may be included in your standard iOS backup. That backup is controlled by Apple under your Apple ID — we do not have access to it.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Flow is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Third-Party Services

Arrow Flow does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App Store itself (Apple) handles app downloads and updates under Apple's privacy policy.

7. Your Rights (UK / EEA Users)

Because we don't collect or hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. To remove all Arrow Flow data from your device, simply uninstall the app.

If you believe your rights under UK GDPR / EU GDPR have been affected, you may contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk/, or your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we ever change how Arrow Flow handles data, we will update this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
