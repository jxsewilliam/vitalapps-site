Frequently asked questions

How do I play Arrow Flow?
Each level is a grid of coloured arrows. Trace a path that follows the arrows, covers every cell on the board exactly once, and ends at the exit. Tap or drag to draw your path. If you go wrong, just drag back to undo.
I'm stuck on a level. What now?
Try tracing backwards from the exit instead of forwards from the start — most levels have one tight corner that gives the whole puzzle away. If you'd rather skip ahead, tap the level select screen and choose another level — you don't have to solve them in order.
Is there a hint system?
Yes. On any level, tap the lightbulb icon to reveal the next correct move. Hints are free and unlimited — there's no penalty for using them.
Are there ads or subscriptions?
No. Arrow Flow is free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. We don't run analytics either.
Does Arrow Flow work offline?
Yes. The game runs entirely on your device. You don't need an internet connection to play.
Will my progress sync between devices?
Progress is stored locally on each device. On iOS, if you have iCloud Backup turned on, your iPhone/iPad backup will include Arrow Flow progress. On Android, your progress is included in your standard Google backup if enabled. Progress doesn't live-sync between devices.
How do I reset my progress?
Open Arrow Flow → Settings → "Reset progress". This wipes your solved levels and best times. You can also uninstall and reinstall the app for a clean slate.
The app crashed or a level looks wrong. What now?
Try fully closing the app and reopening it. If the problem persists, email vitalappsltd@gmail.com with the level number and a screenshot if possible, and we'll fix it.
Where can I read your privacy policy?
See the Arrow Flow privacy policy. Short version: we don't collect anything.
