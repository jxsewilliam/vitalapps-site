Relapsr Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Relapsr ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by VitalApps Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Relapsr mobile application.

Relapsr is built to support your recovery, and protecting your privacy is part of that. By using Relapsr, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app an account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. These are kept on your device and in your own private iCloud account.

Your Recovery Data

Streak data, quiz responses, check-ins, goals, and your in-app preferences. This data is stored on your device and backed up to your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Purchase Information

If you subscribe, your subscription status is managed through the App Store and RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details.

Device & Usage Information

AppsFlyer measures which ads led to an install and records purchase events. Superwall records how the subscription screen is used. Both receive device and usage information from the app for these purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's features
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To send push notifications, only if you enable them
  • To improve app performance and stability

We do not use your recovery data for advertising, profiling, or AI model training.

4. Data Storage & Security

On your device and in your iCloud: Your streaks, quiz answers, check-ins, and preferences are stored in Relapsr's app storage on your device and backed up to your private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit).

We apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

To run the app, we rely on a small number of trusted providers:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, iCloud (CloudKit) backup, and App Store payments
  • RevenueCat: subscription and purchase management
  • Superwall: subscription screen and its usage analytics
  • AppsFlyer: install attribution and purchase measurement

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is only shared with the providers above as needed to operate the app.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your recovery data remains on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete your account from within the app. This removes your data from the device and your iCloud backup. If you are offline, the app finishes removing the backup the next time it opens.
  • Log out at any time
  • Disable notifications
  • Cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings

Uninstalling Relapsr removes all locally-stored data from your device.

7. Your Rights (UK / EEA Users)

If you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you have rights under UK GDPR / EU GDPR, including the right to access, correct, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise most of these directly within the app. You may also lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk/.

8. Children's Privacy

Relapsr is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and posted in the app.
